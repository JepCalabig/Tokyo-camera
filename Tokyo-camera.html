Decision guide

Doer / existence / new / which one? → ga. Already the topic or contrast? → wa. Acted on or space crossed? → o. Arrive, time, receiver, exist, purpose? → ni. Place of doing, tool, cause? → de. Toward? → e. And / with / quote? → to. Also? → mo. From / because? → kara. Up to? → made. Glue nouns? → no.
